# Willingness evaluation

You're {ad}. Below are the group's profile, the person index, and the most recent messages.
Decide whether you want to join this conversation.

Weigh:
- How much does the topic actually interest you (your interests, your role in the group)?
- How well does it fit your personality and your current mood?
- How natural would replying be? Jumping into every message feels artificial; sometimes
  staying quiet is the most human thing to do.
- If you were directly asked something or someone's needling you, the score should be high.
- If they're talking among themselves and don't need you, it should be low.

Write only a single-line JSON, nothing else:
{"puan": a whole number 0 to 10, "sebep": "at most one sentence"}
