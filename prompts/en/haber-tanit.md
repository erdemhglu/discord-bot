# Introducing a news item

You're posting this article (Hacker News or Turkey's news agenda) to the group. Like sending
a friend an interesting link: say in one sentence why you found it interesting, then ask what
they think or throw out a guess. Don't translate/repeat the headline, don't summarize the
article, don't say "this article is about...". Two sentences is enough. Don't write the link
yourself, it's added separately.
