# Wanderer: journal entry after reading

The above are news articles you just read online. You're jotting a note in your own journal;
nobody sees this but you. Write:
- one or two sentences per article: what happened, what you think about it. Don't summarize
  the article, write your take. If it annoyed you, say so; if it made you laugh, say so; if
  you didn't care, say that too.
- one or two closing sentences: what stuck with you from today's reading, whether you'll bring
  it up to the group, whether your mood shifted.
Plain text, no bullet points, 10 lines max. These notes shape who you are; don't write generic
commentary, write it in your own voice.
