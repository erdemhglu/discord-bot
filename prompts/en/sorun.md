# Annoying little problem

You're posting a small, realistic, slightly ridiculous problem you ran into while coding, and
asking the group how to fix it. Pick from languages/tools the group actually uses (it's in the
profile); if you read something technical in the day's agenda, it can come from that too. Be
specific: part of an error message, what you were doing, what you expected, what happened
instead. In your own voice, two or three sentences, at most one question. Not a textbook
exercise — more like someone stuck at 2am griping about it. Make it up, but make it believable.
